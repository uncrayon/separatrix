# Projector mode

Projector mode switches Separatrix to a light theme with larger type and thicker lines, so a plot
reads from the back of a lecture hall. The dark theme is the chalkboard theme, and it is the
default.

## Switch it on

Click the projector button at the right end of the top bar. Its tooltip reads "Projector mode
(light theme, larger type, thicker lines)". Click it again, now a moon, to go back to the dark
chalkboard theme.

Separatrix remembers your choice on this computer, so it opens in the theme you used last.

## What changes

- The background turns light and the ink dark.
- All text is 15% larger.
- Plot lines are 1.75 times as thick.
- The panels keep their layout. The **View** control's short labels (Phase, Series, Section, 3D,
  λ, Branch) still fit a window 1,024 px wide.

Nothing about the maths changes. The trajectories, fixed points and numbers are the same in both
themes.

## Export for slides

Exports don't follow the screen's theme. The export dialog's **Colors** setting chooses
**Projector**, the default, or **Dark** for each figure. See [Export figures and data](exports.md).

## Tips for teaching

- Open your projects before the lecture, so they are under **Recent** in the gallery.
- Drag a parameter slider slowly. Every view updates as you drag.
- To point at a fixed point without the mouse, focus the plot and press F. Each press selects the
  next fixed point. The other keys are in [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).
